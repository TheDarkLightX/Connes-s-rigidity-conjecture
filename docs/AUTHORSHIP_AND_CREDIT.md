# Authorship, credit, and responsibility

This note distinguishes research contribution, scholarly authorship, legal
copyright authorship, publication responsibility, and tool disclosure. It is a
transparency policy, not legal advice.

## Repository credit statement

> **Research direction, tool provision, repository stewardship, and
> publication:** Dana Edwards.
>
> **Research assistance:** OpenAI language models assisted with mathematical
> exploration, proof drafting, formalization, experiments, retrieval, and
> explanatory text.
>
> **Verification:** claim specific. A result is labeled Lean checked only when
> its complete dependency graph passes the pinned build.

The repository does not preserve an independently verifiable receipt for a
more specific model variant. Public material therefore uses the broader and
supportable label "OpenAI language models."

## Why the model is not an author

Scholarly authors must approve the final work, answer for its integrity, and
accept responsibility for corrections. A language model cannot perform those
human responsibilities. It is disclosed as a research tool rather than listed
in an author byline.

Relevant publisher policies include:

- [Nature Portfolio authorship policy](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/authorship)
- [Elsevier generative AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/publishing-ethics-books/generative-ai-policies)

## Human contribution and accountability

Dana Edwards contributed the problem selection, research direction, custom
tools, constraints, critical feedback, publication decision, and repository
stewardship. Those contributions are credited precisely. The repository does
not imply that its steward manually derived or independently verified every
proof.

A future paper needs human authors who substantially review the mathematics,
approve the manuscript, and accept responsibility under the selected venue's
policy.

## Copyright boundary

The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes human-authored expressive elements
from material generated without sufficient human control. Human selection,
arrangement, modification, and independently authored material may be
protectable even when purely generated expression is not.

- [U.S. Copyright Office AI report announcement](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html)
- [U.S. Copyright Office AI initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)

Copyright status and scholarly authorship are separate questions. The
repository license grants permissions to the extent applicable rights exist.

## Evidence labels

- **Generated:** produced with a language model or automated program.
- **Finite verified:** exhaustively checked only over a named finite domain.
- **Lean checked:** the statement and complete dependency graph pass the pinned
  build and project trust audit.
- **Expert reviewed:** a named qualified person has reviewed the statement,
  proof, scope, and literature position.
- **Published by Dana Edwards:** responsibility for making the material public,
  not a blanket claim of manual derivation.
