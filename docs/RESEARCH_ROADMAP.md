# Research roadmap

1. Make the repaired standalone Lean build green.
2. Formalize the prime-field pure-power interpolation lemma and the
   stable/critical divided-power cyclicity theorem, recovering the ternary and
   multivariate files as special cases.
3. Generalize the rank-three coordinate proof to the stable/critical
   polynomial-tensor orbit theorem for arbitrary tensor degree and rank.
4. Construct the uniform prime functional carry, discrete dual extension, and
   formalize the Bockstein exact sequence
   `0 → V/t^nV → A_n[p]/pA_n → ker d_p → 0`.
5. Formalize the classical proof that the abelian subgroup is the amenable
   radical of the semidirect product; then promote the finite-orbit cardinal
   to a machine-checked abstract group invariant.
6. Package the cyclicity/orbit consequences into finite generation and ICC.
7. Formalize both the binary rank-three `1/12` chart detector and the odd-prime
   primitive detector in the Cornulier–Tessera relative-property-(T) transfer.
8. Formalize the product-Haar and Fourier crossed-product proof giving common
   group factors and full/reduced group C*-algebras.
9. Formalize analytic Hopf-defect stability and investigate common group/C*
   algebras.
10. Generalize or falsify the prime--rank phase diagram beyond the established
    stable/critical range, especially ranks `3<=r<p` at odd primes.
11. Audit the binary amenable-radical simplification against every use of the
    published torsion-free acting group, especially the Margulis hypotheses
    and the direct ICC/relative-property-(T) replacements for factor transfer.
12. Obtain and audit a stable version of the current adversarial invalidity
    manuscript against the actual public constructions.
13. Obtain independent modular-representation, group-theory, and
    operator-algebra review before a complete prime-family claim.
