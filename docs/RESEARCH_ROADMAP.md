# Research roadmap

The standalone Lean graph and trust audit are green. The next work begins at
the boundary between the checked rank-three results and the proposed general
theorems.

1. Formalize the prime-field pure-power interpolation lemma and the stable and
   critical divided-power cyclicity theorem.
2. Generalize the rank-three coordinate proof to arbitrary tensor degree and
   rank, preserving the critical symmetry hypothesis explicitly.
3. Construct the uniform prime functional carry, discrete dual extension, and
   Bockstein exact sequence
   `0 -> V/t^nV -> A_n[p]/pA_n -> ker(d_p) -> 0`.
4. Prove that the abelian subgroup is characteristic, for example by a checked
   amenable-radical argument, before treating the finite-orbit cardinal as an
   abstract group invariant.
5. Derive finite generation and ICC from the completed cyclicity and orbit
   results.
6. Formalize the binary rank-three chart detector and the odd-prime primitive
   detector in the relative-property-(T) transfer.
7. Formalize continuity, product Haar measure, Pontryagin duality, and the
   common crossed-product construction.
8. Stress-test the proposed simplification of the binary construction against
   every use of the auxiliary torsion-free acting group.
9. Search modular divided-power, hyperalgebra, current-group, and strict
   polynomial literature for the proposed stable and critical theorems.
10. Obtain independent review from specialists in modular representation
    theory, geometric group theory, and operator algebras before claiming a
    complete prime-uniform family.

Each unfinished item remains an explicit premise or target. Successful finite
examples and adjacent Lean theorems do not promote it automatically.
