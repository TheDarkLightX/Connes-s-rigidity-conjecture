# Theorem status

This repository separates three distinct states:

- **Present:** a theorem statement and proof term exist in the repository.
- **Builds:** the module compiles in the current standalone project.
- **Promoted:** the full dependency chain builds and the mathematical specification has been reviewed as matching the intended claim.

A theorem must satisfy all three conditions before this repository presents it as an established result.

## New proof candidates in the current research packet

- For every prime `p`, every finite-variable polynomial ring over `F_p`, and
  every rank `r>=p` with `r>p` or `p` odd, the divided `p`th-power Frobenius
  kernel and the whole divided power are cyclic. Exact stable/critical
  certificate ledgers pass at primes two, three, and five.
- For tensor degree `2<=m<=r`, every nonzero polynomial tensor has infinite
  elementary orbit when `r>m`; at `r=m`, first-two-factor symmetry suffices.
  The existing Lean source covers only the rank-three critical instance.
- On the minimal-rank line `r_p=max(p,3)`, the cyclicity/orbit theorems and the
  detector phase diagram combine with the uniform Witt carry into a candidate
  prime-indexed family. The intrinsic cardinal is `p^(r_p n)`.
- Scalar length-two Witt identities have been exhausted at `p=2,3,5,7`; the
  general functional carry and Pontryagin-dual construction remain
  formalization obligations.
- The divided-cube Frobenius kernel over `F₃[t]` is cyclic under
  `E₃(F₃[t])`, generated by the all-distinct constant tensor. The proof uses a
  characteristic-three finite difference on an equal-source tensor and
  divisibility comparability of polynomial monomials.
- The entire divided cube is cyclic, generated by the pure cube `e₁^⊗3`.
- The same two cyclicity conclusions extend to
  `F₃[t₁,…,t_d]`; exact bivariate and trivariate quotient oracles pass.
- The finite-orbit subgroup of `A_n[3]/3A_n` is `V/t^nV`, hence has order
  `3^(3n)`, at the `Γ`-module level. Classical normal-subgroup and
  property-(T) theorems identify `A_n` with the amenable radical, promoting
  this to an abstract group invariant once the Bockstein model is constructed.
- The formal orbit statement strengthens from divided cubes to every nonzero
  tensor fixed by the first-two-factor swap.
- The published binary-carry construction already establishes the
  product-Haar/common-crossed-product architecture. The repository supplies a
  ternary specialization giving common group von Neumann, full group C*, and
  reduced group C*-algebras at paper-proof level.
- The proposed semidirect products are finitely generated and ICC at
  paper-proof level: cyclicity gives finite module generation, and the trivial
  amenable radical kills the FC-center.
- The shift-forgetting quotient gives finite-index embeddings
  `G_0 -> G_n` of index `3^(3n)`, so the ternary candidate family is mutually
  commensurable at paper-proof level.
- A joint invariant-measure argument gives a `1/8` clopen escape bound on the
  compact dual. Property (T) of `Γ` upgrades asymptotically invariant measures
  to nearby invariant ones, yielding relative property (T), and hence property
  (T), for the proposed semidirect products at paper-proof level.
- Two detector geometries cover every prime in every rank `r>=3`. Primitive
  boxes have constant
  `c_prim(p,r)=((p-1)/p^2-p^(1-r))/(1-p^(1-r))`; affine charts have
  `c_chart(p,r)=(r-p)(p-1)/(rp^2)` when `p<r`. This recovers Zhou's binary
  rank-three `1/12` and the ternary rank-three `1/8`; an exact chart-restriction
  oracle verifies the sharp block-support count in six boundary cases.
- A candidate simplification of the published binary proof replaces its
  torsion-free congruence-kernel acting group by `SL_4(F_2[t])` itself. The
  abelian layer is recovered as the amenable radical, after which the published
  action-level arguments apply unchanged.
- An untwisted `F`-linear cubic retraction over a general
  characteristic-three field is impossible unless Frobenius is the identity;
  the correct general target is Frobenius-twisted.

The cyclicity and finite-orbit arguments are complete paper proof candidates,
not yet Lean theorems. See the three new notes under `research/notes/`.

## Research claims represented by the imported modules

The repository contains formalization attempts and partially validated proof chains for:

- reconstruction of a group from canonical group-algebra group-like elements;
- bialgebra reconstruction and quantitative Hopf rounding;
- rank-three divided-cube orbit growth under polynomial transvections;
- ternary Witt carries and shifted abelian extensions;
- Frobenius-diagonal and multiplication-by-three identities;
- a ternary degree-three minimum-support argument;
- primitive-vector, dual-shift-kernel, and proposed finite-invariant arithmetic.

These are research targets, not blanket publication claims, until their exact standalone dependency chains are green and independently reviewed.

## Current claim boundary

OpenAI's August 2026 binary-carry manuscript and Shuoxing Zhou's independent
arXiv:2608.02327 supply two public claimed counterexamples. A 1 August
PhilArchive manuscript disputes them, but its public abstract addresses
different groups and leaves a Bernoulli-action inference unbridged. The shared
measured-action idea, common-factor mechanism, shifted finite-orbit strategy,
primitive detector, and binary affine-chart detector are prior art. This
repository is an unreviewed prime-uniform generalization and possible source of
new divided-power theorems and simplifications, not a priority claim for the
counterexample.
