# Current-algebra portal and a Hilbert-series falsifier

Status: **exact combinatorial Hilbert series plus conjectural representation-theoretic interpretation**.

## 1. Total-degree grading

Let

```text
V = F[t]^3
```

over a field of characteristic three. Give `t^n e_i` degree `n`. Let

```text
B = span { v^⊗3 | v ∈ V }.
```

Using the multiset basis of three polynomial basis vectors, the bivariate generating function is

```text
∑_{m≥0} H_{Γ^m(V)}(q) z^m = ∏_{n≥0} (1-zq^n)^(-3).
```

Taking the coefficient of `z^3` gives the exact Hilbert series

```text
H_B(q)
  = (10 + 8q + 8q² + q³) / ((1-q)(1-q²)(1-q³)).
```

The first graded dimensions are

```text
10, 18, 36, 55, 81, 108, 145, 180, 225, 271, ...
```

The denominator is the symmetric-polynomial denominator for three particles. This strongly suggests that `B` is the characteristic-three divided-power analogue of the current-algebra global Weyl module with highest weight `3ω₁`. This identification is a portal hypothesis, not yet a theorem in the repository.

## 2. Frobenius quotient

The Frobenius diagonal

```text
d₃ : B -> V
```

sends the diagonal multiset `(t^n e_i)^⊗3` to `t^n e_i` and kills every non-diagonal multiset.

For compatibility with tensor total degree, place `t^n e_i` in quotient degree `3n`. The image therefore has Hilbert series

```text
H_im(q) = 3 / (1-q³).
```

Consequently the kernel `K=ker d₃` has the exact Hilbert series

```text
H_K(q)
  = H_B(q) - 3/(1-q³)
  = (7 + 11q + 11q² - 2q³) / ((1-q)(1-q²)(1-q³)).
```

Its first graded dimensions are

```text
7, 18, 36, 52, 81, 108, 142, 180, 225, 268, ...
```

Every coefficient is nonnegative even though this particular rational numerator has a negative `q³` term.

## 3. Falsifier for the naïve global-Weyl identification

The constant component `K[0]` is the seven-dimensional `psl₃` module described in `CONSTANT_KERNEL_PSL3.md`. It is tempting to identify the full current-algebra kernel with the standard global Weyl module of highest weight

```text
θ = ω₁ + ω₂.
```

The Hilbert series warns against this identification.

`H_K` has a pole of order three at `q=1`, and its graded dimensions grow quadratically. In the usual characteristic-zero global-Weyl picture, a module of weight `ω₁+ω₂` is controlled by two spectral variables and has pole order two. Thus the kernel is too large to be the naïve `W(ω₁+ω₂)` object.

The safer interpretation is:

> `K` is a cyclic modular current-algebra submodule of the divided-power/global-`3ω₁` object, generated in constant degree by the `psl₃` vector, with additional three-particle spectral freedom inherited from the ambient module.

This statement remains conjectural until the module relations and universal property are established.

## 4. Exact-sequence target

The representation-theoretic object to formalize is not an isolated seven-dimensional module but the graded exact sequence

```text
0 -> K -> Γ³(F³[t]) --d₃-> F³[t]^(Frob,regraded) -> 0.
```

Questions:

1. Is `Γ³(F³[t])` the hyper/current-algebra global Weyl module for `3ω₁` in characteristic three?
2. Is `K` the submodule generated by the all-distinct weight-zero vector?
3. What defining relation distinguishes `K` from the ordinary global Weyl module of weight `θ`?
4. Does `K` have a Weyl/Demazure filtration whose graded character explains the numerator `7+11q+11q²-2q³`?
5. Can the finite `N` cyclicity computations be recovered from a quotient by the ideal of symmetric spectral polynomials vanishing on `t^N`?

## 5. Literature portal

Relevant surrounding theories include:

- global and local Weyl modules for current algebras;
- hyper current algebras in positive characteristic;
- fusion products and Demazure flags;
- modular `SL₃` Weyl-module homomorphisms;

The searches performed so far did not locate this exact Frobenius-kernel sequence or its cyclic polynomial-ring statement. That absence is not proof of novelty.
