Current mathematical lead

A better coordinate for the ternary carry

The naive radix coordinate a + 3b fails as soon as a nontrivial carry appears. The stronger candidate replaces the residue with its cubic Teichmüller lift:

φ(a,b) = a³ + 3b   (mod 9)

Over representatives a ∈ {0,1,2}, the cubic values are 0,1,8 modulo 9. This is exactly the lift needed for the length-two ternary Witt addition law.

Status: LEAP and Julia test the complete declared finite model. A finite exhaustive result is evidence for the scalar system, not a replacement for the Lean theorem or the final property-(T) construction.

Smallest falsifier

Why a + 3b is wrong

  1. (1,0) + (1,0) = (2,1) under the ternary Witt carry.
  2. The naive coordinate sends the sum to 2 + 3 = 5.
  3. But the two input coordinates add to 1 + 1 = 2 modulo 9.

The cubic coordinate sends (2,1) to 2³ + 3 = 11 ≡ 2.

Research method

Representation search, then independent gates.

Changes the representation of a stuck obligation before weakening the mathematics: explicit phantom parameters, canonical coordinates, extensionality, support invariants, and decomposition.

02

Accepts a bounded abstraction only when static safety, non-vacuity, exact transition semantics, portal certificates, and original-domain lifts pass.

03

04

05

Stores claims, counterexamples, evidence, dependencies, uncertainty, and rejected promotion attempts. Missing evidence is non-supporting.

06

Lean

The final gate. The public site reports the complete clean-checkout build, not isolated files or stale incremental success.

Research map

Three layers of the program

A

Hopf reconstruction

An ordinary algebra may forget which group generated it. The canonical coproduct restores the group-like unitaries and therefore the multiplication law.

B

Rank-three algebra

The fresh fourth coordinate used in the binary rank-four argument is replaced by a two-branch proof: ordinary fresh-coordinate separation and all-three-coordinate exponent separation.

C

Ternary construction

Shifted divided-cube carries, Frobenius diagonal, detector bounds, primitive density, intrinsic 3-torsion, property (T), ICC, and common crossed products.
