New proof simplification candidate

The binary proof may not need its auxiliary torsion-free group.

The published construction lifts the action through a specially built torsion-free group so that the abelian layer equals the torsion subgroup. Instead, take Q=SL₄(F₂[t]) directly. Higher-rank lattice theory gives Rad_am(Q)=1, and therefore

Radam(A ⋊ Q) = A

New proof candidate · 4 August 2026

One proof now covers binary rank three and every odd prime.

Let Kp be the Frobenius kernel in the divided pth power of Fp[t₁,…,td]r. When r>p, an unused coordinate lowers every positive-degree basis tensor. On the critical diagonal r=p, odd characteristic supplies the exact two-source identity

(uᵢⱼ(x)S − uᵢⱼ(−x)S)/2 = A(x,y,z) + A(xy,1,z)

The source contains exactly two mutable factors, so antisymmetrization cancels the zero- and two-substitution terms. A total-degree induction proves that the kernel is generated by [e₁,…,ep], and inclusion-exclusion makes the whole divided power cyclic from e₁⊗p.

A companion theorem proves infinite elementary-transvection orbits for every nonzero tensor in degree m<r, and on the critical line m=r whenever the first two tensor factors are symmetric. Exact certificates pass at (p,r)=(2,3),(2,4),(3,4),(5,5),(5,6), in addition to the deeper ternary checks.

Status: complete paper proof candidates with replayed finite certificates. Only the rank-three critical orbit theorem currently has Lean source; specialist review and general formalization remain required.

cprim = ((p−1)/p² − p^(1−r))/(1 − p^(1−r))   ·   cchart = (r−p)(p−1)/(rp²)

Prime-uniform finite-orbit specialization

Recover the shift from finite conjugacy, not chosen coordinates.

The discrete dual extension has Bockstein βₙ=tⁿdp. Its canonical p-torsion subquotient fits into the exact sequence

0 → V/tⁿV → Aₙ[p]/pAₙ → ker(dp) → 0
01

Finite kernel

V/tⁿV has exactly p^(rpn) elements, so every orbit in this term is finite.

02

Infinite quotient

Every nonzero element of ker(dp) has infinite elementary-transvection orbit by the stable/critical theorem.

03

Intrinsic recovery

The finite-orbit subgroup is exactly V/tⁿV. Margulis's normal-subgroup theorem and property (T) identify Aₙ as the amenable radical, so its order is an abstract group invariant after the Bockstein construction is installed.

Prime-uniform specialization of the published carry mechanism

The cocycle changes the group, but not the probability action.

Every compact carry group has the same carrier L×Q. Its product Haar measure and coordinatewise Γ-action are independent of the shift, even though the cocycle changes the compact group law.

L(Aₙ ⋊ Γ) ≅ L∞(L × Q) ⋊ Γ ≅ L(Aₘ ⋊ Γ)
01

Same topology

Carry coordinates are continuous finite-coordinate polynomials, so every shift gives a compact abelian group on the same product space.

02

Same Haar measure

A triangular cocycle translation preserves product measure by two applications of translation invariance and Fubini.

03

Same completions

Pontryagin Fourier transform gives the same von Neumann crossed product and the same full and reduced group C*-crossed products.
