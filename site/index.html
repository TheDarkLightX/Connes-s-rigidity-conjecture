Context

Two constructions reported counterexamples to the broad ICC property-(T) form of Connes rigidity in 2026: an OpenAI binary-carry construction and an independent construction by Shuoxing Zhou. Their counterexamples and common-factor mechanisms are prior work. The mathematics reported here concerns reusable algebraic components and possible prime-uniform extensions.

Main mathematical discovery

A stable and critical cyclicity pattern

Let \(p\) be prime, let

\[ \begin{aligned} R&=\mathbf F_p[t_1,\ldots,t_d],\\ V&=R^r,\\ B_p(V)&=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbf F_p} \left\{v^{\otimes p}:v\in V\right\},\\ B_p(V)&\subseteq \Gamma^p(V). \end{aligned} \]

Write \(E_r(R)\) for the elementary subgroup. Let \(d_p:\Gamma^p(V)\to V\) be the Frobenius diagonal map, put \(q=e_1^{\otimes p}\), and, when \(r\ge p\), let \(w=[e_1,\ldots,e_p]\) denote the all-distinct orbit sum.

Candidate 1. Divided-power cyclicity

If \(r\ge p\), and either \(r>p\) or \(p\) is odd, then

\[ B_p(V)=\Gamma^p(V), \]
\[ \ker(d_p) =\left\langle E_r(R)\mathbin{\cdot}w\right\rangle_{\mathbf F_p}, \]
\[ B_p(V) =\left\langle E_r(R)\mathbin{\cdot}q\right\rangle_{\mathbf F_p}. \]

Status: written candidate with exact finite certificates. The general statement is not yet Lean checked. The argument does not settle the corner \((p,r)=(2,2)\).

Why there are two cases

  1. Stable range, \(r>p\). Among the \(r\) coordinates, an unused coordinate is available. An elementary transvection can lower the total polynomial degree of a positive-degree orbit sum.
  2. Critical range, \(r=p\) with \(p\) odd. An all-distinct tensor uses every coordinate, so no unused coordinate remains. Antisymmetrizing two transvections isolates the required linear term.

The critical identity

For the all-distinct target \(A(x,y,z_3,\ldots,z_p)\), choose a source \(S_y\) with exactly two mutable \(j\)-labelled factors. The difference at parameters \(x\) and \(-x\) cancels the terms with zero or two substitutions:

\[ L(x):=\frac{u_{ij}(x)S_y-u_{ij}(-x)S_y}{2}. \]

The isolated linear contribution is

\[ \begin{aligned} L(x)={}&A(x,y,z_3,\ldots,z_p)\\ &+A(xy,1,z_3,\ldots,z_p). \end{aligned} \]

Division by \(2\) is the reason this branch requires odd \(p\). The second term is reduced by the repeated-label induction, while the source has lower total degree. This yields a simultaneous degree induction.

A companion tensor-orbit statement

Let \(F\) be a field, \(R=F[t]\), \(V=R^r\), and \(2\le m\le r\).

Candidate 2. Polynomial tensor orbits

For every nonzero \(x\in V^{\otimes_F m}\):

  • if \(m<r\), the \(E_r(R)\)-orbit of \(x\) is infinite;
  • if \(m=r\) and \(x\) is fixed by swapping the first two tensor factors, the \(E_r(R)\)-orbit of \(x\) is infinite.

Status: the critical case \(m=r=3\) is Lean checked. The arbitrary-rank statement is a written candidate.

In the stable case, a coordinate absent from a nonzero tensor block exposes a term whose degree grows with the transvection parameter. In the critical symmetric case, the two surviving terms occupy disjoint exponent regions once the parameter degree is large enough.

Prime-uniform consequence under the remaining hypotheses

Define the proposed minimal rank by

\[ r_p=\max(p,3). \]

If the candidate cyclicity theorem, tensor-orbit theorem, and remaining carry and characteristic-subgroup identifications all hold, then the proposed family has the intrinsic finite-orbit count

\[ \left|\operatorname{FinOrb}\!\left(A_n[p]/pA_n\right)\right| =p^{r_p n}. \]

This conditional formula predicts:

  • a binary rank-three family distinguished by \(2^{3n}\);
  • an odd-prime rank-\(p\) family distinguished by \(p^{pn}\);
  • within each fixed prime, common von Neumann, full group \(C^*\), and reduced group \(C^*\)-algebras.

These are conditional consequences, not established conclusions of the current formal development. The group-theoretic and operator-algebraic interfaces listed below remain open.

fixed_swapFirstTwo_SL3_orbit_infinite
Every nonzero element of \((F[t]^3)^{\otimes_F 3}\) fixed by swapping its first two factors has an infinite orbit under an explicit tail of elementary transvections. More precisely, for suitable \(i\ne j\) and \(N\),
\[ \left\{u_{ij}\!\left(t^{N+n}\right)\mathbin{\cdot}w:n\ge0\right\} \quad\text{is infinite}. \]
linear_cube_retraction_forces_frobenius_fixed
An \(F\)-linear map satisfying \(d(x^3)=x\) for every scalar forces \(x^3=x\) for every \(x\in F\). Thus a general characteristic-three construction requires a Frobenius-aware target.
ternaryShiftedCarry_nine_nsmul
The shifted ternary carry extension has exponent dividing nine. The checked chain also characterizes its three-torsion and gives the shifted-kernel cardinality \(3^{3n}\) in the formal coordinate model.

A checked negative result

Refuted formulation

An untwisted \(F\)-linear cubic retraction cannot be assumed over every field of characteristic three.

\[ d(x^3)=x \quad\text{and }F\text{-linearity}\quad \Longrightarrow \quad x^3=x. \]

This rules out the earlier general-field formulation. It does not by itself construct the corrected Frobenius-twisted map.
